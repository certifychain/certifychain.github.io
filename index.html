Decentralized Certification Platform

Welcome to CertifyChain, an open-source, decentralized certification platform built on blockchain technology. Our mission is to create a transparent, secure, and community-driven platform for issuing, verifying, and managing digital certificates.

CertifyChain enables organizations to issue digital certificates to individuals, ensuring authenticity and integrity. By leveraging blockchain, certifications are recorded in a secure, transparent, and tamper-proof way.

We believe certification should be community-driven and free from centralized control. Decentralization ensures certificates are immutable and auditable, while individuals retain full control over their credentials.

Certificates are hosted via IPFS as NFTs, decentralizing storage and resisting censorship and single-point failures.
